Attractions

The landmarks, beaches, and events that make Winneba worth exploring — start here before you plan your day.

Winneba Beach

A wide, breezy stretch of coastline popular for evening walks, football games, and watching local fishing boats come in.

Open daily
Photo placeholder

Aboakyer Festival

An annual deer-hunting festival celebrated by the people of Winneba, marked by processions, drumming, and durbar of chiefs.

Every May
Photo placeholder

Winneba Lagoon

A quiet spot on the edge of town, good for bird-watching and a slower-paced afternoon away from the main streets.

Open daily
Photo placeholder

Winneba Town Market

The heart of daily life in Winneba — fresh produce, local goods, and a good place to get a feel for the town.

Mornings recommended
