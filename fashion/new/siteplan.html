JOBEQS Thread and Trend

Website Site Plan

01 · Site Name

JOBEQS Thread and Trend

The name JOBEQS honors the founder's parents, Joseph and Rebecca (JO + BE), with the letters QS standing for Quality and Style. "Thread and Trend" was added to the business name to speak directly to what the brand does — working thread by thread (tailoring, sewing, craftsmanship) to create pieces that set the trend rather than just follow it. The logo itself pictures a dress form wound with red thread alongside scissors and pins, so the site's color schema is pulled straight from it.

jobeqsthreadandtrend.com

02 · Site Purpose

Purpose

The site serves as the digital home of JOBEQS Thread and Trend, giving visitors a place to browse the brand's ready-to-wear, bridal, casual, corset, and corporate collections, learn the story and values behind the brand, and book a fitting or custom-order appointment. Quick-access WhatsApp and Call buttons keep enquiries fast and personal, matching how the brand's customers already prefer to reach a boutique — directly and in conversation, not through a long contact form.

03 · Scenarios

Visitor Scenarios

  • Bride-to-be, browsing on TikTok I love the bridal pieces JOBEQS posted online — how do I book a fitting and find out if they do custom bridal designs?
  • Returning customer, on the go I need a quick answer about sizing before I order — is there a fast way to chat with someone right now instead of filling out a form?
  • First-time visitor Who is behind this brand, and what makes JOBEQS different from every other clothing page I see online?

04 · Color Schema

Color Schema

The palette is pulled directly from the JOBEQS logo — the red thread wound around the dress form, the black mannequin silhouette, and the white/silver outline — so the site reads as one continuous brand from the icon to the last page.

#171414 — Mannequin Black Headings, header/footer background, nav text
#C8102E — Thread Red Primary buttons, accents, dividers, active links
#8C0B20 — Deep Red Hover / pressed states, eyebrows, secondary accents
#FFFFFF — White Page background, logo backdrop
#C9C7C4 — Silver Borders, dividers, pin/scissor metal accents
